A VILLAGE, a small settlement rather than a single building: a humble little camp of four or five SMALL low shelters, canvas tents on timber frames and simple lean-tos of cloth and poles, clustered loosely around a bare dirt clearing with a small campfire ring and a low drying rack. Everything is small, temporary and hand-made, no taller than a person. Rough undyed canvas, weathered timber poles, rope and packed earth, with realistic cloth and timber materials.
