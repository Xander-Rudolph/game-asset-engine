A BARRACKS: a squat military drill hall built of heavy dark grey stone blocks with a low pitched roof of black slate, narrow slit windows barred with iron and no warm light, an iron-banded door, a crenellated parapet along one side, and a bare packed-earth drill yard in front holding wooden weapon racks of spears and a straw practice dummy. A banner on an iron pole. Severe, fortified and military, with realistic stone and iron materials.
