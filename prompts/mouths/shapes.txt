# Edit instructions for scripts/make_mouths.py, one per mouth shape.
#
# The letters are Rhubarb Lip Sync's: X is rest, A to F are the six basic
# shapes, G and H are the optional two. A shape starts with its letter and a
# colon; indented lines continue it. Lines starting with # are ignored.
#
# Each instruction names the one change, gives a spoken sound to anchor it,
# and ends with what must stay the same, because the edit graph re-diffuses
# the whole picture and only the mouth box of each result is kept.

X: Change only the mouth. Close the lips softly so they rest together without
   any pressure, with the corners level, as on a face that is silent and at
   ease. Keep the eyes, eyebrows, nose, cheeks, jaw line, hair, skin tone,
   lighting, clothing, framing and background exactly the same, and do not
   move, turn or resize the head.

A: Change only the mouth. Press the lips together firmly into a flat line, as
   just before saying the B in "bob": the lips look thinner than at rest
   because they are pressed against each other, the skin just above and below
   them is slightly tensed, and no teeth show. Keep the eyes, eyebrows, nose,
   cheeks, jaw line, hair, skin tone, lighting, clothing, framing and
   background exactly the same, and do not move, turn or resize the head.

B: Change only the mouth. Part the lips a little while the upper and lower
   teeth stay closed together, so a narrow row of clenched teeth shows between
   the lips, as when saying the S in "yes" or the EE in "see". Keep the eyes,
   eyebrows, nose, cheeks, jaw line, hair, skin tone, lighting, clothing,
   framing and background exactly the same, and do not move, turn or resize
   the head.

C: Change only the mouth. Part the lips slightly, as when saying the short
   vowel in "bed": the jaw lowers just a little, the lips stay relaxed and keep
   their width, the edges of the upper front teeth show, and below them there
   is a narrow dark gap about as tall as those teeth. Keep the eyes, eyebrows,
   nose, cheeks, hair, skin tone, lighting, clothing, framing and background
   exactly the same, and do not move, turn or resize the head.

D: Change only the mouth. Open the mouth wide, as when saying "ah" in "father":
   the lips part into a tall opening that shows the upper teeth and the tongue
   lying low in the mouth, but not as wide as a shout or a yawn. Keep the chin
   and jaw line where they are as far as the open mouth allows. Keep the eyes,
   eyebrows, nose, cheeks, hair, skin tone, lighting, clothing, framing and
   background exactly the same, and do not move, turn or resize the head.

E: Change only the mouth. Shape the lips into a slightly rounded opening, as
   when saying the vowel in "law" or "her": the mouth is open less than for
   "ah", the corners draw in so the opening looks rounder, and only a little
   of the teeth shows. Keep the eyes, eyebrows, nose, cheeks, jaw line, hair,
   skin tone, lighting, clothing, framing and background exactly the same, and
   do not move, turn or resize the head.

F: Change only the mouth. Push the lips forward into a small, tight pucker, as
   when saying the "oo" in "moon" or the W in "we": the corners pull in towards
   the centre and the lips leave only a small round opening with no teeth
   showing. Keep the eyes, eyebrows, nose, cheeks, jaw line, hair, skin tone,
   lighting, clothing, framing and background exactly the same, and do not
   move, turn or resize the head.

G: Change only the mouth. Tuck the lower lip up and slightly inwards so the
   upper front teeth rest on it, as when starting to say the F in "fish": the
   upper teeth show, the lower lip sits just under their edges and hides the
   lower teeth, and the jaw is almost closed. Keep the eyes, eyebrows, nose,
   cheeks, jaw line, hair, skin tone, lighting, clothing, framing and
   background exactly the same, and do not move, turn or resize the head.

H: Change only the mouth. Part the lips and teeth a little and curl the tip
   of the tongue up inside the mouth so it presses against the ridge behind
   the upper front teeth, as when holding the L in "hello": the upper teeth
   show, the pink underside of the raised tongue is seen through the gap
   below them, and the tongue stays inside the mouth behind the teeth. Keep
   the eyes, eyebrows, nose, cheeks, hair, skin tone, lighting, clothing,
   framing and background exactly the same, and do not move, turn or resize
   the head.
