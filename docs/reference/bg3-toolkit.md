# Baldur's Gate 3 Toolkit

::: tip Status: researched on 2026-10-03, projection script built and run on a stand-in
What this pipeline's output would have to become to ship as a character mod through Larian's official Toolkit and in-game Mod Manager, and what the terms allow. Read: the game EULA, the BG3 Fan Content Terms, the older Modding Terms, Larian's forum guidelines, mod.io's terms and acceptable use policy, the official wiki page by page (as raw wikitext), LSLib's source and release archive, the Blender exporter's source, and Hunyuan3D 2.1's licence text. Run: LSLib's Divine under Wine on this host, listing and extracting from the game's `Models.pak`, converting the halfling bodies to glTF and the projected bodies back to conformed GR2; `scripts/bg3_project.py` on those bodies in the container's Blender on the CPU. Not run: the Toolkit, any import into the Toolkit, any publish. The claims are in [research/claims/bg3-toolkit.json](https://github.com/Xander-Rudolph/game-asset-engine/blob/main/research/claims/bg3-toolkit.json) on GitHub.

## The short answer

- **The generated mesh never enters the game.** BG3 animates characters on Larian's own skeletons, with Larian's topology, UVs and material layout, and the wiki's contract for a mesh is topological: clean loops, the naked body's topology under anything skin-tight, identical vertex order across race variants. <!-- BG3-016 BG3-018 --> A TRELLIS or Hunyuan3D mesh is triangle soup with its own atlas, so it serves as a **shape and colour source**: Larian's base mesh is deformed onto it in Blender, keeping its own vertices, weights and UVs, and the colour is baked across. `scripts/bg3_project.py` does that and writes the three maps BG3 materials expect. <!-- BG3-021 BG3-033 -->
- **Armour and body meshes enter the Toolkit as `.gr2`; only hair is shown importing from `.fbx`.** The GR2 is written by LSLib's Divine from a glTF or Collada file made with Norbyte's Blender exporter. <!-- BG3-014 BG3-015 BG3-027 BG3-028 -->
- **Everything runs on this Linux host.** The game itself is a native Linux build here (`bin/bg3` is an ELF executable among Linux shared objects, and its profile, Mods folder and `modsettings.lsx` live under `~/.local/share/Larian Studios/Baldur's Gate 3/`); the Toolkit is installed through Steam (Proton); Divine runs under Wine with a Windows .NET 8 runtime, because its native build crashes on any Unix path and the game's compressed GR2 files need `granny2.dll`, a Windows library; the container's Blender does the projection and bakes. Larian's own pages say Windows only for the Toolkit; Steam Play and Wine are the difference there. Whether the Toolkit itself runs to the point of importing a model was not checked here. <!-- BG3-009 BG3-027 BG3-035 BG3-041 -->
- **A published mod is worldwide, so Hunyuan3D is out of it.** The publish step grants Larian and mod.io worldwide distribution, Larian lists PC, Mac, Xbox and PS5 for the game and the Mod Manager offers the author platform opt-ins but no region control, and Hunyuan3D 2.1's licence forbids displaying its Output outside a territory that excludes the EU, the UK and South Korea. The clean route is a Qwen-Image concept, a TRELLIS shape (MIT) and a colour from anything but the Hunyuan3D texture stage. <!-- BG3-002 BG3-006 BG3-011 BG3-012 BG3-013 -->
- **Mods are free, yours only where original, and Larian's where derived from the game.** No selling; donations and public commissions are the exceptions the Wizards policy allows. No document on the official route mentions AI-made assets. <!-- BG3-002 BG3-003 BG3-005 BG3-032 -->
- **Heads are undocumented.** The wiki covers armour, weapons, hair and beards; nothing on making or importing a head, its facial rig, morphs or neck seam. Ship a body first and keep Larian's heads, where the character creator's aging and skin options live. <!-- BG3-020 -->

## The route, step by step

1. **Get the base mesh out.** Either in the Toolkit, "Override in the Active Mod..." on the body VisualResource, which puts its `.gr2` in your mod's Data folder, or with Divine straight from `Models.pak`: `dotnet Divine.dll -g bg3 -a list-package -s Models.pak`, then `extract-single-file` and `convert-model -i gr2 -o glb`. Divine's native .NET 8 build fails on Linux with `This operation is not supported for a relative URI` on any path, so it runs under Wine 10 with Microsoft's Windows x64 .NET 8 runtime unpacked beside it and `granny2.dll` from the release zip copied next to `dotnet.exe`, where it is found; that read the game's compressed GR2 files here (`WINEPREFIX=~/.wine-lslib wine dotnet.exe Divine.dll -g bg3 -a list-package -s 'Z:\...\Models.pak'`, then `extract-single-file` and `convert-model -i gr2 -o glb`). <!-- BG3-024 BG3-026 BG3-027 BG3-035 --> Keep what comes out under `MODELS_DIR/bg3_library/`, outside the repository, as the Daz library is kept. <!-- BG3-034 -->
2. **Make the shape.** Concept with Qwen-Image, shape with TRELLIS, as [the pipeline](/guide/asset-workflow) does. Ask for the pose the base has, arms clear of the body and feet apart, or the wrap folds limbs onto the wrong surface. Keep Hunyuan3D out of anything that will be published. <!-- BG3-013 -->
3. **Project.** `scripts/bg3_project.py BASE.glb SOURCE.glb --out output/bg3/<name>` scales the source so its floor-to-shoulder height matches the base's (the base's shoulder is a bone; the source's is read off its outstretched arms), stands it on the base's floor, turns it to face the base's way (both read from the feet), cuts its head off above the base's neck line, turns its limbs onto the base's rest pose segment by segment in 3D (upper arm, forearm, thigh, shin, foot) by borrowing the base's weights, wraps the base and its LODs onto a voxel remesh of the source with a Shrinkwrap, removes the part of each move that runs along the vertex's bones so limbs keep the base's proportions and bend where its joints are, averages the move by position so facets do not print through and shell seams stay closed, holds the neck ring by position (`--seam`) and `--lock` groups by weight, rebuilds the normals from the moved faces, bakes the source's colour onto the base's UVs as BM, tangent normals as NM and ambient occlusion into PM's blue channel with flat metal and roughness, and writes the deformed base with its armature as glTF carrying LSLib's `EXT_lslib_profile` metadata, as FBX, with `--dds` as DXT5 DDS maps, plus a JSON record with the share of texels no ray reached. The base's weights, UVs and vertex count never change. <!-- BG3-033 BG3-036 BG3-037 BG3-038 -->
4. **Dress the maps.** Convert the three TGAs to DDS (nothing in the image writes DDS; the Toolkit converts BM, NM and PM into one Virtual Texture on import), add an MSK if the body is to be dyeable. <!-- BG3-021 -->
5. **Export GR2.** `bg3_project.py` writes the `EXT_lslib_profile` scene and mesh entries that Divine's glTF importer requires, copied from the base's glTF, which Divine wrote, so no Blender add-on is needed: `Divine -g bg3 -a convert-model -i glb -o gr2 -s <name>.glb -d <name>.gr2 --conform-path <original .GR2>` wrote the halfling bodies here, and converting a result back to glTF kept all five LODs, the 82 joints and every vertex attribute. Without those entries Divine fails with `Object reference not set to an instance of an object`. Norbyte's Blender exporter (GPL-3.0-or-later, Blender 3.6 and later, glTF in 4.5 and 5.0, Collada only to 4.5) remains the route for a mesh made in Blender by hand; its own GR2 button shells out to `divine.exe` in a way that does not start a program on Linux. <!-- BG3-027 BG3-028 BG3-036 -->
6. **Import and wire.** Source Asset Data Path, Add Resource, Model; Slot ID Body; material from a base-game one; Edit Visual; then the character-creation tables for the race. <!-- BG3-022 BG3-023 -->
7. **Publish.** Publish Local for a `.pak` to test; Publish to mod.io after accepting Larian's and mod.io's terms; wait for the automated scan; Go live. Console curation is opt-in and re-tested on every update. <!-- BG3-008 BG3-010 -->

**Or skip the Toolkit for a local test.** `scripts/bg3_pack.py --name <Name> --replace HFL_F_NKD_Body_A.GR2=<body>.gr2` stages each GR2 at the virtual path the vanilla one has in `Models.pak` (read from Divine's listing), writes `Mods/<Name>/meta.lsx`, packs the tree with Divine's `create-package` (lz4) and lists it back; `--install` copies the pak into the game's Mods folder beside the mod.io downloads (`~/.local/share/Larian Studios/Baldur's Gate 3/Mods` for the native build here, the same path inside the Proton prefix for a Steam Play install), and `--enable` adds a `ModuleShortDesc` entry for it to `modsettings.lsx` after backing that file up, in the shape the game writes for its own entries. The game reads a file from the highest-priority pak that carries its path, so the new body replaces the vanilla one wherever it is used, with no new resources and no character-creation tables. That mechanism, and the `meta.lsx` layout the script writes, are the modding community's practice, not something read in Larian's documents here, and the pak had not been loaded in the game when this was written. Under Proton the Toolkit's file dialogs see the Steam library as drive `S:` and the Linux root as `Z:`, so the game's Data path is `S:\steamapps\common\Baldurs Gate 3\Data` and this repository's output is under `Z:\asset-engine\output\bg3`. <!-- BG3-039 BG3-040 -->

## What was run

On 2026-10-03, on the reference machine, in the container's Blender 4.5.9 on the CPU, with no Larian file involved: `transfer_weights.py` put the fighter's 28-bone UniRig skeleton onto its textured 39,986-face mesh to make a stand-in base with UVs and weights; `bg3_project.py` then deformed that base onto the basilisk's textured mesh and baked its colour, normals and AO at 1024 px in 3.4 s, keeping all 28 groups and bones. The shapes were deliberately unalike, mean vertex move 0.159 of a 2.0 height, and the bakes showed it: large unreached regions in the normal map, so the script now reports the share of texels no ray reached and defaults to a longer ray. The matched pairs followed the same day, below.

Also on 2026-10-03: three halfling shapes were made for this target through the pipeline, `output/mesh/halfling_nimble.glb`, `halfling_m.glb` and `halfling_f.glb`, 48,000 triangles each from TRELLIS, from Qwen-Image concepts approved by the owner.

Later on 2026-10-03, on the game's own files. Divine's native .NET 8 build fails on Linux on any path, so it ran under Wine 10 with Microsoft's Windows x64 .NET 8.0.31 runtime and `granny2.dll` beside `dotnet.exe`: it listed `Models.pak` (8.9 GB), extracted `HFL_F_NKD_Body_A.GR2`, `HFL_M_NKD_Body_A.GR2` and the two `HFL_*_Base.GR2` files, and converted them to glTF into `MODELS_DIR/bg3_library/`. <!-- BG3-035 --> The female body is 9,390 vertices, 16,130 faces, 82 vertex groups and an 82-bone armature with four LODs; it is forty shells, and its 24-vertex neck ring is weighted to the chest and scapula bones, not the neck, which is why the script holds that ring by position rather than by weight. <!-- BG3-037 -->

Four matched projections taught the script its present shape. Span alignment put the source's feet below the floor and height alignment cut its arms off, so the ruler is now floor to shoulder: the base's shoulder bone against the arm line read off the source. The first sources, Hunyuan3D meshes of the clothed concepts qwenfast_00012 and 00015 (linen undershirt, short trousers), wrapped their hems and boots into the body, so six bodysuit T-pose takes were generated with `txt2img_qwen_fast.json` (seeds 101 and 202, three each, `output/concept/qwenfast_00016` to `00021`), and 00017 (female) and 00019 (male) were chosen here, by Claude and not by the owner; Hunyuan3D 2.1 made their meshes in 67 s and 49 s (`output/mesh/halfling_f_suit.glb`, 19,898 vertices and 39,960 faces, and `halfling_m_suit.glb`). Corrective Smooth tore every shell seam, because it walks each shell's own edges, so the smoothing now averages the displacement field by position, which keeps coincident seam vertices together. <!-- BG3-038 -->

Those runs were wrong in a way the clay renders hid and the owner saw at once in a viewer: the seat was on the front and the limbs read as mangled. The Hunyuan3D figures stand facing -Y and Larian's bodies face +Y (toe bone minus ankle bone, +0.116 and +0.128), so the wrap had put the base's front onto the source's back. The script now reads each figure's facing from its feet (the toes reach further forward of the shin than the heel reaches back; -0.022 and -0.005 on the sources) and turns the source round by matrix, because the glTF importer leaves objects in quaternion mode, where an Euler turn is ignored. The exported normals were also still the game's own, 2.9% of them more than 30 degrees off the moved surface against 1.0% on the untouched body; they are now rebuilt from the moved faces and averaged across the shell seams (1.6% and 1.4% afterwards). <!-- BG3-038 -->

The final runs (`--lock Finger --lock-weight 0.3 --seam 0.1 --cut-margin 0.03 --size 1024 --samples 2 --dds`): female scale 0.6161, shoulders turned 21.1 and 20.1 degrees, hips 13.9 and 14.8, ankles 20.8 and 21.3, wrap mean move 0.0202 of the 1.068 height, max 0.0874, 3,216 finger vertices held; male scale 0.6385, shoulders 26.5 and 27.5, hips 10.2 and 11.5, ankles 17.5 and 19.6, mean move 0.0190 of 1.134, max 0.1156, 3,171 held; normal maps 0.0% unreached in both. Judged by eye in clay renders (`output/bg3/halfling_f_v9_preview_big.png`, `halfling_m_v8_preview_big.png`) and against the source in the `_check_side.png` renders: a slim, continuous body facing the base's way, with clean arms, hands and legs and a smooth neck; the feet are small where the source's are. Divine wrote conformed GR2 files from the glTF (`halfling_f_v9.gr2`, 928,268 bytes, and `halfling_m_v8.gr2`, 867,964 bytes) once the script put LSLib's `EXT_lslib_profile` metadata back; a GR2 to glTF round trip of an earlier run kept all five LODs, 82 joints and every vertex attribute. ImageMagick 7.1.2 wrote the baked maps as DXT5 DDS (1,398,256 bytes each at 1024 px, the size of a DXT5 chain with mipmaps). Not run: the Toolkit. <!-- BG3-036 BG3-038 -->

Packed the same evening: the halfling mod's pak (`output/bg3/<Name>.pak`), 1,567,339 bytes and three files (the two GR2 files at their vanilla paths under `Generated/Public/Shared/Assets/Characters/_Models/Halflings/`, and `Mods/<Name>/meta.lsx`), written by `bg3_pack.py` through Divine's `create-package` under Wine and read back by `list-package`. The game here is a native Linux build with five mod.io paks already in `~/.local/share/Larian Studios/Baldur's Gate 3/Mods/`; the pak was copied there with `--install`, and `modsettings.lsx` (version 4.8.0.700) showed how the game lists an enabled mod, a `ModuleShortDesc` node with Folder, MD5, Name, PublishHandle, UUID and Version64, which `--enable` writes. It was not yet enabled or loaded. <!-- BG3-039 BG3-041 -->

The owner then loaded it: the game took the pak from its Mods folder and the halfling bodies changed, which settles the override route, but the arms and legs were mangled in motion and the arms looked squashed down. The skeleton was not the cause: Divine's glTF of the GR2 carries bind matrices, bone transforms and hierarchy identical to the original's to five decimals, and the same weight per bone. A posed test told the rest: posing the vanilla body and the projected one identically in Blender (shoulders down 55 degrees, elbows bent 40, one hip forward 45 with the knee bent 70) showed thin arms kinking at the elbow, a boxy step where the vanilla-sized hand met the thinned forearm, and a crease on the bent thigh. Two faults in the script. The pose match had aligned limbs in the front plane only, while the game's rest pose bends the forearm about 18 degrees forward as well as down, so the base's forearm skin had wrapped onto a source arm behind it; it now aligns each segment in 3D, parent first (female: shoulders 29 and 30 degrees, elbows 11 and 13, hips 17 and 19, knees 20 and 22, feet 28 and 31, with 3.6 degrees or less left). And a nearest-point wrap slides skin along a bone towards a shorter limb, which bunches it at the joints; the part of each move that runs along the vertex's bones is now removed (5.8 cm per vertex on the female, 2.5 cm kept across), and the whole hand is held (`--lock Finger,Wrist --lock-weight 0.5`). In the posed test the new bodies bend at the elbows cleanly and the hands join the forearm; the bent thigh still creases. The owner judged the posed results bad and paused the automated route on 2026-10-03 to merge the shapes onto the bodies by hand in Blender; the fresh pak was built and not installed. `--blend` saves the lined-up scene for that hand work (armature, weighted body and LODs, the shape posed and unposed, the remeshed target), and `_check.glb` now carries the same with the skin. The pieces that hold: Divine under Wine both ways, the `EXT_lslib_profile` writer (`write_lslib_profile` in `bg3_project.py` works on any GLB exported from Blender), the pack script, and the posed test as a check before the game. <!-- BG3-042 -->

## Licences

- **mod.io.** A perpetual worldwide licence to mod.io and Larian, UGC made public through the API, your warranty that you hold the rights, removal at Larian's discretion first. The terms' date is unsettled in the register: a browser render showed "Last updated: Sep 2, 2024", and the static document the page renders prints no date. <!-- BG3-006 BG3-007 -->

## What not to do

- Do not import a TRELLIS or Hunyuan3D mesh into the Toolkit as a body; project onto Larian's base instead. <!-- BG3-016 BG3-018 -->
- Do not publish anything that passed through Hunyuan3D, shape or texture. <!-- BG3-012 BG3-013 -->
- Do not sell or paywall the mod, or ship it with new shader files if consoles matter. <!-- BG3-003 BG3-005 BG3-022 -->
- Do not put a Larian mesh, a converted GR2 or a baked map of Larian's textures in the repository, the image or an AI stage. <!-- BG3-034 -->
- Do not redistribute LSLib's release zip; fetch it. <!-- BG3-026 -->
- Do not read the Blender exporter's GR2 button as working on Linux; call Divine yourself. <!-- BG3-028 -->

## What to build

1. The owner's hand merge in Blender: export the merged body as GLB with Blender's exporter, put the profile back with `write_lslib_profile`, convert with Divine `--conform-path`, pack with `bg3_pack.py`, install, and look at a halfling moving. If the automated route is picked up again, its open faults are the thigh crease in the posed test and whatever the game shows that the posed test does not; or import `halfling_f_v9.gr2` and `halfling_m_v8.gr2` through the Toolkit under Proton (Add Resource, Model; Slot ID Body; a base-game material; Edit Visual; the halfling character-creation tables), then Publish Local. Record what happened either way. The Toolkit's authentication wizard is the owner's step.
2. Check which DDS block formats the game's body maps use, and whether the Toolkit converts on import; the script writes DXT5.
3. An MSK map, if the Toolkit's material needs one beyond the vanilla body's.
4. Judge the bodies in motion, and the feet, which follow the source's small ones.
5. A `--base` option on the pipeline's graph is not possible: the projection needs Blender, which no ComfyUI node in the image runs; the script is the stage.

## Honest uncertainty

- Whether the Toolkit under Proton imports a model and publishes is unverified here; Larian says Windows only. <!-- BG3-009 -->
- Whether the Toolkit accepts a GR2 that Divine wrote from this glTF route: Divine reads it back; the Toolkit has not seen it. <!-- BG3-036 -->
- Whether the re-projected bodies move cleanly in the game: the first pak loaded and changed the bodies, which confirms the override route, but its limbs were mangled, and the fix has only been seen in a posed Blender test. <!-- BG3-040 BG3-042 -->
- The baked maps are optional: the projected body keeps Larian's UVs, so the game's own BM, NM and PM apply to it; the DDS formats BG3 expects were not checked. <!-- BG3-038 -->
- Which Larian document the Toolkit's authentication wizard presents could not be read; the Fan Content Terms and the Modding Terms disagree on nothing substantive but differ in detail. <!-- BG3-002 BG3-003 -->
- No document answers whether projecting an AI shape onto Larian's mesh is "prompt ... generative AI tools using Larian Studios IP". <!-- BG3-032 -->
- The wiki calls itself unreviewed by Larian, and the 17 pages read here were all last edited by one user. <!-- BG3-014 -->
- The reference FBX downloads and Larian's body skeletons were not opened; bone names, units and axes are unknown. <!-- BG3-017 -->
- The matched projection, halfling onto halfling, has not been run.
