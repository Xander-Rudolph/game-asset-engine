# Animation sources

::: tip Status: researched on 2026-10-02
Every way read so far to put an animation cycle on this repo's rigs: clip libraries, retargeting add-ons, Blender's own tools, text-to-motion models, video-to-motion models and hosted services. Nothing was installed into the image, no clip was moved onto a rig, no model ran and no hosted export was made. What ran: `scripts/bone_roles.py` on two downloaded skeletons, Blender's BVH importer in the container on ACCAD files, a file-level read of the Mixamo clips the image ships, and a host-side port of one add-on's rig detection over the four unit rigs. Everything else is read: licence files at named commits, model cards, terms pages, add-on source. The 290 claims behind this page, each checked through the lenses `research/README.md` describes, are in [research/claims/animation-sources.json](https://github.com/Xander-Rudolph/game-asset-engine/blob/main/research/claims/animation-sources.json) on GitHub. [Video to 3D motion](/reference/video-mocap) covers the capture models in depth and was re-checked the same day.
:::

The pipeline now rigs a character with UniRig's `articulationxl` template, whose bones are `bone_0` to `bone_N` with a different count per figure, and [animation](/guide/animation) poses them by role. Nothing yet puts a clip from outside onto such a rig: UniRig's Apply Animation node refuses anything without `mixamorig:` names, which was run on 2026-09-30. <!-- ANI-065 ANI-201 --> This page is about closing that gap, for a game that will be sold.

## The short answer

- **The cleanest route is free clip libraries plus a retarget keyed on bone roles, and the retarget is the one thing to settle first.** Quaternius's two Universal Animation Libraries are CC0 1.0, with the licence file inside the zip, 43 cycles at 30 fps in each free GLB; `bone_roles.py map` already names every role on their 65-bone skeleton. <!-- ANI-058 ANI-062 ANI-063 ANI-066 --> ACCAD's Open Motion Project is CC BY 3.0, 304 BVH files with no account, and its Female 1 set is a T-pose at 30 fps. <!-- ANI-191 ANI-195 ANI-196 ANI-197 --> The CMU database may be included "in commercially-sold products" but not resold "even in converted form", and 100STYLE is CC BY 4.0 with 810 BVH files. <!-- ANI-043 ANI-049 ANI-179 ANI-185 -->
- **Three GPL Blender add-ons already do most of that retarget**, by hierarchy or by role slots a script can fill: Retarget BVH 5.2.0, Rig Bridge 0.1.66 and Expy Kit 0.6.1. None was run here; a port of Retarget BVH's rig detection named all 21 roles on the four unit rigs. Trial them before writing one. <!-- ANI-069 ANI-077 ANI-204 ANI-213 ANI-219 ANI-229 -->
- **Video capture now has one open route whose every layer read permits commercial use**: NVIDIA's GEM-X on the SOMA body model, Apache-2.0 code and body model, weights under the NVIDIA Open Model License, and a model card that says it was trained only on NVIDIA-owned and synthetic data, with no AMASS (the card's statement, not checked). It needs its own Python 3.12 environment, states no GPU memory figure, and its BVH is a 78-joint SOMA skeleton that still needs the retarget. <!-- ANI-120 ANI-121 ANI-124 ANI-129 ANI-132 ANI-133 -->
- **Text-to-motion is not clear as released.** HY-Motion 1.0, the only one with commercial terms and ComfyUI wrappers, forbids using or distributing its Output outside a Territory that excludes the EU, the UK and South Korea. MoMask, MDM and MotionStreamer are trained on AMASS-derived data whose licence forbids training "for commercial use of any kind", and MDM cannot be built without the non-commercial SMPL files. <!-- ANI-100 ANI-104 ANI-252 ANI-262 ANI-268 ANI-283 ANI-290 -->
- **Hosted riggers replace UniRig rather than feed it.** Meshy, Tripo and Mixamo rig a mesh on their own skeleton and animate only that; DeepMotion SayMotion will retarget onto an uploaded rig on a paid plan, binary-only. None animates a rig it did not make from a video. <!-- ANI-014 ANI-029 ANI-153 ANI-163 ANI-176 -->

## The retarget every route needs

Every source above and below ends at the same step: a clip on a named skeleton must land on `bone_0` to `bone_N`, whose count changes per figure and whose rest pose has the arms hanging, while Quaternius, CMU and ACCAD Female 1 rest in a T-pose. <!-- ANI-055 ANI-062 ANI-198 --> Three Blender add-ons were read for it, none run:

| Add-on | Licence | Finds bones by | Rest pose | Headless |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Retarget BVH](https://github.com/Diffeomorphic/retarget_bvh) 5.2.0, by Thomas Larsson | GPL-2.0-or-later, a 15-line notice, no licence text in the zip <!-- ANI-069 --> | hierarchy and geometry, no names needed; a port of its `findArmature` named all 21 roles on the four unit rigs and failed on the three-root golem, which a `known_rigs` JSON would cover <!-- ANI-076 ANI-077 --> | puts both rigs into a T-pose by bending, and keys the difference <!-- ANI-078 --> | by `bpy.ops.mcp.load_and_retarget` with the rig active, from the user scripts path <!-- ANI-075 ANI-079 --> |
| [Rig Bridge](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/humanoid_remap_studio/) 0.1.66, by Feng Ji Shi | GPL-3.0-or-later by its manifest and README, with the plain GPLv3 text as its LICENSE; the register holds this claim as unsettled, because its second check refuted details of which packaged files changed, not the licence <!-- ANI-204 --> | presets, semantic names, or 37 role slots a script can fill <!-- ANI-210 ANI-214 --> | a rest-delta copy, gated on hips, head and feet being where a standing figure has them <!-- ANI-211 ANI-212 --> | through scene properties and operators, read not run; its canvas imports `gpu` and `blf` at import time <!-- ANI-213 --> |
| [Expy Kit](https://github.com/pKrime/Expy-Kit) 0.6.1, by Paolo Acampora | GPL-3.0 stated in one file header, no licence file; its second check found one function adapted from Blender's GPL-2.0-or-later rigify, so the register holds the dependency claim as unsettled <!-- ANI-219 ANI-220 --> | a preset pairing the two rigs' bone names, which the roles file can generate <!-- ANI-225 ANI-229 --> | left to the user: bind in a matched pose <!-- ANI-226 ANI-229 --> | bake with `do_bake=True` and `exclude_deform=False`, or every deforming bone is skipped <!-- ANI-227 ANI-230 --> |

GPL section 2 covers output only when it is itself a covered work; a keyed Action holds no add-on code, so the clips are yours. <!-- ANI-072 ANI-207 ANI-222 --> Whether any of the three registers under the windowless pip `bpy` 4.5.9 is the most likely point of failure and is untested.

The image's own Blender can also build a cycle from the repo's role poses with no retarget at all: key the compiled poses a few frames apart, close the loop with the Cycles F-curve modifier, pin feet with an IK constraint, bake with `nla.bake`, and export with the bundled FBX or glTF writer, which sample the evaluated pose. <!-- ANI-089 ANI-091 ANI-092 ANI-093 ANI-096 --> Whether four held poses interpolate into a readable walk at 220 px was not tried. Procedural Walk, a GPL-3.0 add-on, does not pin feet by reading: its IK trackers are captured from its own FK result, and its per-side foot overrides are dead code on `bone_N` names. <!-- ANI-231 ANI-241 ANI-243 ANI-249 -->

## Video to motion: GEM-X

[GEM-X](https://github.com/NVlabs/GEM-X) is NVIDIA's video-to-motion model on the SOMA body, released 2026-03 and read at `3299255`. By layer: code Apache-2.0; weights at `nvidia/GEM-X`, ungated, under the NVIDIA Open Model License, which permits commercial use, claims no ownership of outputs, and is revocable and amendable, with an Apache-2.0 LICENSE file in the same repository as a second statement; SOMA-X and MHR Apache-2.0; the SAM 3D Body checkpoint it runs as a frozen feature extractor under Meta's SAM License, redistributed ungated by NVIDIA; the DINOv3 backbone code fetched unpinned by `torch.hub`; and a YOLOX-X detector trained on HumanArt, a dataset released "for non-commercial purposes", left open here. <!-- ANI-120 ANI-121 ANI-122 ANI-124 ANI-125 ANI-140 ANI-143 --> Its card says it was trained only on NVIDIA-owned and synthetic data, with no real footage of people and no AMASS; that is the card's statement, not checked. <!-- ANI-133 -->

It takes one video of one person, re-encodes it at 30 fps, and writes per-frame SOMA parameters and, with `--retarget`, a 78-joint SOMA-named BVH; the BVH export imports modules that exist only at the pinned `soma-retargeter` commit. <!-- ANI-128 ANI-129 ANI-130 ANI-144 ANI-146 --> It wants Python 3.12 and torch 2.10 with CUDA 12.6, about 6.7 GB of weights, and names an A100 80 GB as its benchmark hardware with no memory figure for inference. <!-- ANI-132 ANI-148 --> Its sibling GEM-SMPL moved to NVIDIA's non-commercial licence on 2026-05-30. <!-- ANI-126 --> A Wan 2.2 clip from the pipeline, at 16 fps and 480x640, has not been tried on it.

## Hosted riggers

Meshy's Rigging API builds Meshy's own skeleton on a GLB and its Animation API animates only that rig; the terms since 2026-09-19 give paid customers their output, keep the preset clips as "Service Assets", and let Meshy train on non-Enterprise input and output. Its pages say Mixamo bone names, but no page lists them and no export was made. <!-- ANI-001 ANI-003 ANI-004 ANI-007 ANI-010 ANI-014 --> Tripo's API rigs for 25 credits and retargets a preset for 10, with ownership of output for "Paid Users", a term its agreement does not define for pay-as-you-go credits, and terms changeable "AT ANY TIME"; ComfyUI 0.30.2 already ships its rig and retarget nodes. <!-- ANI-015 ANI-016 ANI-025 ANI-026 ANI-029 --> Mixamo's auto-rigger gives the `mixamorig:` names UniRig's Apply Animation wants, but its clips reach only a `mixamo`-template rig, which [rigging](/guide/rigging) advises against for generated figures. <!-- ANI-167 ANI-176 -->

## What not to do

- Do not sell retargeted curves from CMU, RancidMilk, ACCAD or 100STYLE as a clip pack; bake them into the game. <!-- ANI-049 ANI-046 ANI-192 ANI-183 -->
- Do not use orangeduck's `fbx.zip` of 100STYLE: every file embeds the non-commercial Geno mesh. <!-- ANI-181 ANI-190 -->
- Do not read MoMask's, MDM's or MotionStreamer's code licences as clearance; their weights were trained on AMASS-derived data and need SMPL on the way out. <!-- ANI-262 ANI-290 ANI-279 -->
- Do not make HY-Motion clips for a game sold in the EU, the UK or South Korea until Tencent answers whether 6(d) frees Output from 5(c). <!-- ANI-104 ANI-117 -->
- Do not fetch HY-Motion or Qwen weights from the Aero-Ex or SumitMathur8956 mirrors, which redistribute SMPL files, and do not install Text2MotionPrompter, which exhausted a 32 GB card. <!-- ANI-107 ANI-118 ANI-119 -->
- Do not script DeepMotion's portal or pool its free accounts, and do not feed Mixamo clips or characters into any training or evaluation of a model. <!-- ANI-152 ANI-168 -->
- Do not count Meshy, Tripo or Mixamo auto-rigging as animating a UniRig rig; they replace it. <!-- ANI-014 ANI-029 ANI-176 -->
- Do not build the retarget before trialling the three add-ons. <!-- ANI-082 ANI-218 ANI-230 -->

## What to build

None of this is built.

1. **Settle the owner decision** in `research/untested.md`: trial the three retargeters on the unit rigs, and write the Copy Rotation retarget VMC-050 describes only if all three fail.
2. **Trial Retarget BVH 5.2.0** in the image's `bpy` on `unit_rogue` (24 bones, every role traced) with an ACCAD `Female1_B03_Walk1.bvh` and a Quaternius walk exported to BVH; record whether `register()` works windowless, the frame rate, and the Action; fetch it by sha256 at start-up as the entrypoint does for comfyui_controlnet_aux, with a redistributing row and the GPL-2.0 text if it ever goes into the image. <!-- ANI-082 ANI-083 -->
3. If that fails on two rigs, **trial Rig Bridge** with its slots filled from the roles file, then **Expy Kit** with a generated preset. <!-- ANI-218 ANI-230 -->
4. **Make the free Quaternius GLBs the first clip source**, keeping `License.txt` beside them and using the in-place variants for sprite cycles; add ACCAD Female 1 and cgspeed CMU walks as raw takes with a cycle cutter, and the ACCAD and CMU credit lines. <!-- ANI-068 ANI-202 ANI-056 -->
5. In parallel, **key the repo's own role poses as a loop** in Blender and judge it at 220 px; add IK foot pinning only once that reads. <!-- ANI-096 ANI-097 -->
6. **Trial GEM-X** in its own environment on one Wan 2.2 clip resampled to 30 fps with `--static_cam`, measuring peak VRAM, with the `soma-retargeter` submodule at its pinned commit; prefer it to bare SAM 3D Body. <!-- ANI-136 ANI-150 ANI-151 -->
7. Correct [licensing](/guide/licensing) on Meshy's current terms and Mixamo's two conditions, and [rigging](/guide/rigging)'s table, which still sends game humanoids to the `mixamo` template. <!-- ANI-002 ANI-178 -->

## Honest uncertainty

- **Nothing was run** against a rig: no add-on installed, no clip moved, no model inferred, no hosted export made. Whether the pip `bpy` accepts `register()` for any of the three add-ons is the likeliest failure and is untested; whether a T-pose clip lands cleanly on an arms-down rest pose through each one is a reading of code. <!-- ANI-079 ANI-212 ANI-229 -->
- **The paid Quaternius tiers' licence file was not read**; only the free zips' `License.txt` was. The site presents a single Quaternius Asset License without saying which tiers it governs. <!-- ANI-060 ANI-064 -->
- **The `mixamorig:` prefix was seen on no Meshy or Tripo page**, and no export was made, so whether either rig takes UniRig's Apply Animation is documented, not verified. <!-- ANI-010 ANI-023 -->
- **GEM-X's VRAM on a 16 GB card**, whether the host's driver runs its CUDA 12.6 wheels, and whether the YOLOX-X detector's HumanArt training set restricts a commercial pipeline are open. <!-- ANI-148 ANI-143 -->
- **HY-Motion's 5(c) against 6(d)** is an owner or counsel question. <!-- ANI-104 -->
- **Whether a rendered sprite sheet is "embedded" output** under Mixamo's and DeepMotion's terms was not settled; the Mixamo FAQ page answered 403 on the day. <!-- ANI-166 ANI-171 -->
- **Nothing here covers a non-humanoid rig.** Every library, retargeter and model read is humanoid; the golem and the creature families have no route on this page.
- **Two readers covered GEM-X**, so the register holds its claims twice, from different files; they agree.
- Several hosted terms are amendable without notice, so no date line on this page is a change marker. <!-- ANI-016 ANI-152 -->
